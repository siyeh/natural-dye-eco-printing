Natural Dye and Eco Printing — Learning Journey

Welcome to a self-guided tour through the world of natural dyeing and eco printing. This is a practice that has been with us for at least 6,000 years — one that spans botany, chemistry, textile arts, history, and ecology — and yet is completely accessible to a beginner with a kitchen pot and a handful of onion skins. This journey takes you from the big picture of why colour and cloth have always mattered, through the science and materials you need to understand, into hands-on processes, and finally out toward more advanced techniques and the broader questions the craft raises.

Each step is a self-contained note you can read in any order, but the sequence below is designed to build understanding progressively. Read a step, sit with it, try something, then come back. The craft rewards slow, curious engagement more than speed.

Before you touch a dye pot, it helps to understand where this craft sits in the world — its history, its two main branches, and why people are drawn to it today.

Stage 5 — Eco Printing: The Plant as Printer

Eco printing is a distinct branch of the craft in which the plant itself does the printing — no dyebath required. These three notes cover plant selection, the bundling and steaming process, and adapting the technique for paper.
